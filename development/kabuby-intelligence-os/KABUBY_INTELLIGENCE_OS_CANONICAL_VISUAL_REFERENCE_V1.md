# Kabuby Intelligence OS canonical visual reference V1

**Status:** `OWNER_APPROVED`
**Direction:** `LOCKED`
**Implementation status:** `RECORDED_ONLY_FOUNDATION_NOT_VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION`

## Non-negotiable truth boundary

The approved dashboard reference is a visual specification, not production evidence. Its example values must never be hardcoded as Kabuby business facts. A future implementation renders only values from certified Physical read models; otherwise it shows `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_PROVEN`, `STALE`, `BLOCKED`, `PARTIAL`, or `WAITING_FOR_EVIDENCE`.

Priority order: data truth, security, and Scout protection; certified Physical contracts; the owner-approved visual reference; responsive and accessibility needs; developer preference.

## Brand and environment

The Kabuby Intelligence OS uses a gold geometric K plus the `kabuby` wordmark. The compact mark, favicon, app icon, and compact-sidebar mark are the gold geometric K on a dark Kabuby surface. The reference palette is deep navy-black and charcoal-blue surfaces with premium gold, teal/emerald, electric blue, controlled coral risk, and warm-white typography. Avoid generic AI gradients, neon, rainbow dashboards, or gaming visual language.

## Canonical desktop composition

The desktop shell has three zones: persistent navigation at left, intelligence workspace in the center, and a right rail for Kabuby Pulse and Recent Activity. The hierarchy for Today / Command Center is time-aware hero, owner greeting, market summary, quick questions, dominant Top Opportunity, owner-attention cards, business overview, Kabuby Pulse, and Recent Activity. It is a Commerce Intelligence OS, not a generic administration dashboard.

The future canonical navigation is Today, Opportunities, Scout, Supply, Economics & Risk, Marketplace Operations, Portfolio, Intelligence & Learning, and Settings. Scout remains an independent protected surface.

## Time-aware hero

The future client-side hero follows authenticated local time unless a safer canonical timezone is available:

| Daypart | Local time | Greeting | Scene |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | 06:00–11:59 | Good morning, {first_name}. | Mountain sunrise with warm gold and cool shadows |
| Afternoon | 12:00–17:59 | Good afternoon, {first_name}. | Same mountain world in clear daylight |
| Evening | 18:00–21:59 | Good evening, {first_name}. | Mountain sunset with cinematic dusk |
| Night | 22:00–05:59 | Good night, {first_name}. | Deep navy mountain night with restrained celestial light |

Assets must be controlled and versioned with the application; no random external landscape fetching. The four assets must form one coherent mountain visual family. Motion must be subtle, informative, reduced-motion aware, and never imply live system activity that is not supported by freshness/health truth.

## Truthful visual behaviour

Kabuby Pulse, Recent Activity, opportunity cards, owner-attention cards, and business metrics use only canonical events and read-model facts. A `LIVE` indicator requires canonical freshness and health. Quick intelligence questions are disabled or truthfully unavailable until backed by certified read-only intelligence. Unknown or blocked material must not be visually styled as approval or execution.

## Scout hard red line

`SCOUT = PRESERVE_AS_IS_PROTECTED`. A future shell can surround Scout, but may not modify Scout runtime, UI, routes, authentication, contracts, schemas, Chrome extension integration, read models, or behaviour. Scout protection is a mandatory release gate.

## Future implementation acceptance

The future visual implementation must compare against the owner-approved reference for composition, brand mark, sidebar, daypart hero, typography, spacing, Top Opportunity hierarchy, attention cards, Pulse, Recent Activity, color system, and cinematic premium tone. It also requires accessibility, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, sufficient contrast, responsive hierarchy, and reduced-motion support.

No visual implementation is authorized by this record. The prerequisite is certified Waves 0–2 foundation and a separately approved `KABUBY_INTELLIGENCE_OS_VISUAL_IMPLEMENTATION_V1` phase.
