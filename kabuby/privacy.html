Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Kabuby processes information while operating as an internal business-intelligence and market-research platform under development and validation.

What Kabuby is

Kabuby is designed to identify emerging consumer behaviors, recurring problems, unmet needs, workarounds, trends, solution gaps and potential commercial opportunities. It analyzes publicly available information and limited account information that an owner expressly authorizes through supported providers. Kabuby currently operates as an internal research platform and does not offer public user accounts.

Information Kabuby processes

Kabuby may process public product, marketplace, search-interest and public-content metadata made available by authorized providers. The specific fields depend on the provider and research question. Kabuby applies data minimization and does not seek sensitive personal attributes.

Operational security records may include limited technical information needed to protect the service, such as timestamps, request identifiers and error classifications. Credentials and secret values are not intended to appear in public output.

eBay connection through OAuth

If the Kabuby owner chooses to connect an eBay account, the connection uses eBay OAuth. Kabuby redirects the owner to eBay for authorization and requests only the read-only eBay Sell Account and Sell Finances scopes needed for the approved account and fee-intelligence function.

Kabuby does not receive, request, or store the owner’s eBay password, two-factor authentication code, or eBay sign-in session. eBay handles account sign-in and consent. Kabuby receives OAuth tokens only after eBay returns an approved authorization response.

eBay information and purpose

For a connected eBay account, Kabuby may process account subscription information and a bounded set of eBay financial transaction fields needed to understand fees: marketplace, account tier or status where returned, transaction date and type, booking entry, fee type, fee amount, fee basis amount and currency. Kabuby does not persist raw eBay responses, buyer personal information, or order identifiers in its fee-intelligence evidence records.

Kabuby uses this information only to create owner-authorized, read-only account and fee evidence for internal economics analysis. It does not use this connection to publish listings, change prices or inventory, place orders, fulfill orders, contact buyers or sellers, or perform another commercial action.

OAuth token storage, security and retention

OAuth authorization state is server-side, single-use and short-lived. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in Kabuby’s server-side credential storage; they are not placed in Kabuby public pages, browser JavaScript, research output, or sanitized evidence receipts. Kabuby uses HTTPS, backend-only credential handling, restricted provider operations and access controls designed to limit access to the connection.

Kabuby retains the connection credential only for the token lifetime and continued operation of the authorized connection. A connection may be discontinued by revoking it through eBay or by contacting Kabuby. Sanitized, immutable evidence created from a successful read may be retained for operational traceability and audit; it does not contain the OAuth token, raw provider response, buyer personal information, or order identifier.

YouTube API Services

Kabuby uses YouTube API Services as one independent source of public-data research. Through the YouTube Data API v3, Kabuby may access publicly available search results and permitted video or channel metadata. Kabuby does not currently use YouTube OAuth, access private YouTube user information or connect to or manage YouTube user accounts.

Kabuby does not upload, edit, delete or manage YouTube content; manage channels or comments; create artificial engagement; or manipulate YouTube metrics.

Use of YouTube-related functionality is governed, where applicable, by the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How YouTube data is used

Public YouTube API data may be reviewed as evidence of public content, language, topics, problems or workarounds relevant to a bounded market-research question. A public video or search result is an observation, not proof of sales, market demand, user identity, audience demographics or commercial intent.

YouTube is not the sole basis for a Kabuby conclusion. Provider provenance and observation time are retained so YouTube-sourced data can be distinguished from other evidence and from Kabuby’s own analysis.

Data storage and retention

Kabuby may temporarily cache limited public, non-authorized YouTube API data only as needed for its research purpose. Such data is deleted or refreshed within 30 calendar days, and reasonable efforts are used to keep stored API data consistent with current YouTube data.

Kabuby does not currently store YouTube OAuth tokens or Authorized Data obtained on behalf of a YouTube user. If this changes, this policy and the relevant consent and deletion controls will be updated before that functionality is enabled.

Any longer retention of eligible statistical data or derived metrics would occur only if Kabuby’s applicable analytics use case is accepted under YouTube’s additional policies for derived metrics and data storage. Other metadata, including titles, creator names, descriptions and comment text, remains subject to the applicable deletion or refresh requirements.

Derived analysis

Kabuby may create internal market-research interpretations using evidence from multiple sources. Kabuby-generated scores, opportunity classifications, consumer-need interpretations, trend interpretations and commercial conclusions are Kabuby analysis—not YouTube data, YouTube metrics or YouTube conclusions—and are not endorsed or approved by YouTube or Google.

Expanded YouTube-derived metrics are not currently enabled. They will not be enabled unless the applicable YouTube policy and audit requirements have been satisfied.

Other data sources and sharing

Kabuby may use authorized search, marketplace, product-history, public social-data and infrastructure providers. eBay processes OAuth sign-in and consent; Cloudflare provides Kabuby’s hosting and server-side infrastructure. Information is processed by these providers only as needed to operate and secure the service, subject to their terms and applicable law. Kabuby does not sell eBay account information or private YouTube user information.

Your rights and contact

Requests are evaluated under applicable law, provider policies and Kabuby’s operational retention requirements.

Policy changes

This policy may change as Kabuby’s validated capabilities or legal obligations change. Material updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Kabuby will not enable materially different eBay or YouTube data access or retention practices without updating the relevant disclosures and controls.
